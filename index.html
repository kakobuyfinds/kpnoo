How to use an OrientDig spreadsheet

Match the exact listing

Open the HipobuyFind product page and check the available buying links. If an OrientDig option is present, open it and confirm the seller, color, size, and price before paying. Compare the seller’s measurements with a similar item that fits you.

Review warehouse QC photos

When warehouse photos are available for your order, match them to your selected variant. For sneakers, check both size labels and the soles. For clothing, check the print placement, seams, and any measurement photos. Check the agent’s return window before approving shipment.

Estimate the delivered cost

Add the item price, domestic delivery, any service fees, international shipping, and applicable taxes or duties. Compare routes using the destination and the packed parcel’s weight and dimensions.

Frequently asked questions

What is an OrientDig spreadsheet?

It is a product directory that helps shoppers find listings to compare before ordering through a purchasing agent. This guide uses a browsable catalog of selected HipobuyFind listings.

Where does the Buy it now button take me?

It opens the HipobuyFind homepage. Use a product card’s source link to go directly to that item and review the buying options on its listing.

Where can I check QC photos?

The catalog shows source listing photos. For QC photos of an item you ordered, check your agent’s order or warehouse page when the parcel arrives there. Match the photos to your exact color and size.

What does the USD estimate cover?

It covers the listed item price converted to an approximate USD amount at the time of the catalog check. Shipping, agent fees, and any applicable duties or taxes are separate costs.
